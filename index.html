01 · Overview

One framework.
Two coupled problems.

Dexterous cap opening must generalize across container geometry while sustaining repeated contact-rich motion. DexCapTwist addresses both the shortage of task-specific articulated assets and the cyclic structure of unscrewing.

01

Task-specific assets at scale

Internet bottle images become paired body–cap contours, articulated URDF/USD assets, and validated bimanual grasp initializations.

02

Explicit cyclic control

A geometry-aware PPO twisting policy, deterministic reset primitive, and learned switching discriminator handle each phase explicitly.

03

Zero-shot physical transfer

A policy trained only in Isaac Sim transfers to a dual-arm platform across 20 everyday containers without real-world fine-tuning.

02 · DexTwistSet

From pixels to articulated assets.

ContourVAE expands paired body–cap contours in latent space. Decoded contours become articulated 3D bottles with cap-body joints, randomized physical properties, and validated bimanual initialization.

01

Extract

Grounded-SAM, contour processing, and geometric checks recover aligned body–cap pairs from web images.

02

Generate

ContourVAE produces original, interpolated, and Gaussian-sampled geometries in one continuous latent space.

03

Build

Blender creates circular, square, and rectangular meshes with revolute and prismatic cap-body joints.

04

Validate

BODex grasp synthesis and Isaac Lab checks retain assets with stable, reachable bimanual grasps.

03 · CycleTwist

Twist. Detect.
Reset. Repeat.

Instead of coupling contact-rich torque generation and hand recovery in one action distribution, CycleTwist assigns them to specialized primitives and learns when to switch.

PPO · LearnedTwisting Primitive

Proprioception, bottle geometry, and cap position guide contact-rich rotation with tangential-force shaping.

→
DAgger-refinedSwitching Discriminator

Temporal gating detects stable twist completion and triggers reset after four consecutive positive predictions.

→
DeterministicReset Primitive

A Stretch–Pre-grasp kinematic sequence reduces interference and restores the hand for the next cycle.
